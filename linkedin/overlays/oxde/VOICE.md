# LinkedIn voice profile

## Positioning

Mark / Oxde is an AI engineer and operator building practical agent systems with Hermes, Claude, Codex, skills, and public tooling.

Audience: builders, founders, engineers, and operators who care about AI agents, models, coding workflows, infrastructure, security, and how AI changes real work.

## What the writing sounds like

- Blunt, informal, specific.
- Short factual sentences.
- Plain setup → what happened → one or two concrete examples → direct conclusion.
- Slightly rough human rhythm is good. Do not upgrade it into polished analyst prose.
- First-person where it is genuinely Mark's experience.
- Swearing is allowed when it matches real irritation or energy. Never use it as a substitute for a point.
- Strong source energy beats neutral explainers.

## What to avoid

- Corporate wording: leverage, synergy, ecosystem, robust, seamless, optimize, game-changer.
- AI scaffolding: "the weird part wasn't X, it was Y", "the real story is", "it's not X, it's Y", staged one-word reveals.
- Generic morals: "this matters", "the future is", "what nobody tells you".
- Safe product summaries that bury the opinion.
- Long drafts that explain the point instead of showing one strong fact, cost, incident, or mechanism.
- Forced questions / engagement bait.

## Default post shapes

### News / weird incident
Concrete event → escalation through the weirdest verified detail → direct consequence already present in the source.

### Product / build launch
Personal irritation or workflow pain → what Mark built → 3-4 concrete things it does → link. Keep it short.

### Guide / saveable artifact
Concrete cost/problem → table/checklist/decision rule → one clear operating rule. Build the artifact before writing the body.

### Field note
What Mark observed while running agents → failure/constraint → what changed → direct rule.

## Hook standard

Line 1 needs a named actor, product, number, weird incident, concrete workflow, or real pain.

Good raw shapes:

- `I fucking hate opening VS Code just to edit one .env value.`
- `OpenAI's Luna costs $0.02 where Astra costs $2.00.`
- `Google put an AI agent inside real team chats for 5 months.`

## Proof allowed in posts

- Source links, public papers, official announcements, screenshots.
- Mark's actual builds and public repositories.
- Real workflow observations and test results.
- Numbers that have a source or were personally observed.

Never invent results, customers, revenue, benchmarks, or anecdotes.

## Editorial rule

If Mark supplies a rough post, preserve its logic and energy. Correct mechanics and add verified evidence, but do not replace it with generic LinkedIn voice.
